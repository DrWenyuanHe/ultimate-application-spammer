# Changelog

Each section is a release. The update prompt shows the section for the new version, so it is written for people using the app, not for developers.

## 0.2.0 — not released yet

Less asking, better documents.

- Your own documents and answers are used straight away. The assistant no longer asks you to confirm each fact. If two documents disagree, it leaves that fact out and tells you once.
- When you correct something, it is fixed everywhere and remembered, so you don't have to repeat it.
- Stronger resume bullets: the assistant now sees each story, number and caveat when it writes, says what came of your work, and checks the numbers against your records. These checks are notes for the assistant and never stop a document.
- The posting's text is saved with the job, and the assistant starts from the facts that match it.
- Good bullets are kept for reuse, and you can keep several resume angles (for example research and operations).
- New writing rules, 32 in all and all on by default: they push for outcomes, full credit, exact numbers and shorter resumes. Rules you changed, approved or turned off stay as you left them.
- Academic CVs, with published, submitted and in-progress work kept apart.
- Jobs get a rating, and "what should I apply to next?" lists the ones worth acting on. Old checks, passed deadlines and missing required qualifications are flagged.
- The assistant can check that a job link still opens the right posting, spot the same job under different links, and come back to a held job on a date.
- "Am I on track?" works out a weekly pace for your goal.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-25

Fixes found while testing a full application from start to finish.

- PDF export now works when your workspace sits in a long folder path, such as a folder deep inside OneDrive or Documents. Before, the PDF failed with no explanation.
- After an application is submitted, the dashboard no longer shows the old "Submit…" step. It shows "Wait to hear back; follow up if no reply" until you set a follow-up date.
- Clearer steps for submitting through Claude's built-in browser. It cannot attach files, so the assistant asks you to attach the resume and cover letter yourself and checks they are on the form before going on. Claude in Chrome can attach them itself.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25

The first release.

- Talk to Codex or Claude in plain language to set up, find jobs, prepare and submit applications, and keep a record of what was sent.
- A short onboarding saves your details and your answers to the questions application forms usually ask, so the assistant never has to guess.
- Put your old resumes and CVs in one folder and the assistant builds a career library from them, checked twice, with every fact waiting for your confirmation.
- An optional experience interview turns each job into specific examples with numbers and dates.
- A private dashboard on your computer shows every job by stage, with deadlines and next steps. It is for looking; you tell your assistant what to change.
- Find jobs on employer job boards and see why each one was shortlisted, rejected or put on hold.
- Resume and cover-letter templates in Word, with PDF export if LibreOffice (or Word on Windows) is installed.
- The assistant can fill in application forms after you sign in. By default it waits for you to say "submit". It stops whenever a form asks something new.
- An exact copy of every submitted application, with every question and answer, in its own folder for interview preparation.
- Automatic database backups (one a day, taken when the dashboard starts or your assistant first changes something that day), an optional second backup folder, and a clean-up that moves old files aside for you to delete yourself.
- Updates ask you first, back up your database, and can be undone.
- Nothing is ever deleted by the app or the assistant.
