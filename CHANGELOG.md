# Changelog

Each section is a release. The update prompt shows the section for the new version, so it is written for people using the app, not for developers.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25

The first release.

- Talk to Codex or Claude in plain language to set up, find jobs, prepare and submit applications, and keep a record of what was sent.
- A short onboarding saves your details and your answers to the questions application forms usually ask, so the assistant never has to guess.
- Put your old resumes and CVs in one folder and the assistant builds a career library from them, checked twice, with every fact waiting for your confirmation.
- An optional experience interview turns each job into specific examples with numbers and dates.
- A private dashboard on your computer shows every job by stage, with deadlines and next steps. It is for looking; you tell your assistant what to change.
- Find jobs on employer job boards and see why each one was shortlisted, rejected or put on hold.
- Resume and cover-letter templates in Word, with PDF export if LibreOffice (or Word on Windows) is installed.
- The assistant can fill in application forms after you sign in. By default it waits for you to say "submit". It stops whenever a form asks something new.
- An exact copy of every submitted application, with every question and answer, in its own folder for interview preparation.
- Automatic database backups (one a day, taken when the dashboard starts or your assistant first changes something that day), an optional second backup folder, and a clean-up that moves old files aside for you to delete yourself.
- Updates ask you first, back up your database, and can be undone.
- Nothing is ever deleted by the app or the assistant.
