# 3 Templates

The look of your documents and the rules your assistant writes by:

- `resume-format.json` and `cover-letter-format.json`: fonts, sizes and margins. Ask your assistant to change them (for example "make my resume font 10.5 pt"), or edit a value yourself and then ask it to "refresh my templates".
- `Resume master.docx`, `Cover letter master.docx` and `Examples/`: Word versions showing every slot, plus a filled-in fictional example.
- `Format Guide.md`, `Writing Rules.md`, `Review Checklist.md`: how documents are laid out, written and checked.

`Writing Rules.md` is regenerated by the app. To add, change or turn off a rule, tell your assistant.

Your copies here stay on your computer and are excluded from Git.
