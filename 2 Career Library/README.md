# 2 Career Library

Drop your resumes, CVs, cover letters, transcripts, award letters and portfolio pieces here. Older versions help too.

Your assistant reads them to build your career library: your jobs, degrees, projects and achievements, each linked to the document it came from. It uses what your documents say without asking you to confirm each fact. If two documents disagree, it leaves that fact out and tells you once. You can correct anything by saying so.

The app keeps these files on your computer and Git ignores them. Your assistant (Codex or Claude) reads them while it works, under that service's own privacy terms.
