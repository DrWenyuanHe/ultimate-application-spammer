# 2 Career Library

Drop your resumes, CVs, cover letters, transcripts, award letters and portfolio pieces here. Older versions help too.

Your assistant reads them to build your career library: your jobs, degrees, projects and achievements, each linked to the document it came from. Every fact is marked "needs review" until you confirm it (or until it is checked against an official record, such as a degree certificate), and only those facts go into applications.

The app keeps these files on your computer and Git ignores them. Your assistant (Codex or Claude) reads them while it works, under that service's own privacy terms.
