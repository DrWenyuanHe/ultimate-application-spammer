# Disciplines and job titles

Employers use many different titles for similar work. This page groups jobs into **lanes** (kinds of work) and lists, for each lane, the titles you will see, words to search with, and where those jobs are usually posted.

Use it to:

- choose one to three lanes to focus on, instead of searching for everything;
- build job-board searches and alerts (put multi-word titles in quotes, for example `"research coordinator"`);
- recognize a job that fits you even when the title is unfamiliar.

**Titles are only a hint.** Always read the duties and requirements. A "Scientist" at one company may be mostly lab work and at another mostly data analysis. A "Coordinator" may be entry-level or senior.

**Seniority words** appear across all lanes: Assistant, Associate, Junior/I, Specialist/II, Senior/III, Lead, Principal, Manager, Director. "Associate" is often early-career; "Principal" and "Staff" are usually senior.

Abbreviations you will see: HEOR is health economics and outcomes research; CRO is contract research organization; GCP is Good Clinical Practice.

Sector names in _italics_ below match [Employers by sector](Employers%20by%20sector.md). Platforms and boards are explained in [Where jobs are posted](Where%20jobs%20are%20posted.md).

## Research science (mixed lab and analysis)

Designing and running experiments, analyzing the data, and writing it up. Many roles mix hands-on lab work with analysis, planning and reporting; read the duties to see the split.

- **Titles:** Research Scientist, Scientist I/II, Senior Scientist, Research Associate, Associate Scientist, Staff Scientist, Research Investigator, Assay Development Scientist, Translational Scientist, Computational Biologist (more analysis), Lab Manager.
- **Search words:** `"scientist"` plus your field (for example `"scientist" immunology`), `"research associate"`, `"assay development"`, `"translational research"`, `"in vivo"`, `"cell biology"`, `"data analysis"`, `"study design"`.
- **Where to look:** _Biotech & therapeutics startups_, _Pharmaceutical companies_, _Hospitals & research institutes_, _Universities & colleges_, _Scientific instruments & lab tools_, government research organizations. Employer careers pages first; LinkedIn and Indeed second; academic boards for university roles.
- **Tip:** if you want less bench time, look for words like "analysis", "design", "strategy", "data" and "cross-functional" in the duties, and be wary of postings that list only techniques.

## Research programs, grants and research development

Helping researchers win and manage funding, and running research programs: finding funding opportunities, editing proposals, tracking budgets and reporting.

- **Titles:** Research Grants Officer, Research Development Officer/Specialist, Grants Facilitator, Research Program Manager, Research Operations Coordinator, Research Administrator, Scientific Program Manager, Research Funding Specialist, Knowledge Mobilization / Translation Specialist, Research Officer (at funders).
- **Search words:** `"research development"`, `"grants officer"`, `"grant facilitator"`, `"research program manager"`, `"research administration"`, `"knowledge mobilization"`, `"knowledge translation"`, `"research funding"`.
- **Where to look:** _Universities & colleges_ (often on the staff jobs site, under the research office), _Hospitals & research institutes_, _Non-profits, charities & research funders_, _Government & public agencies_. CharityVillage and university career sites carry many of these; fewer appear on LinkedIn.

## Scientific writing and editing

Turning science into clear documents: manuscripts, regulatory documents, education materials, grant text and marketing content.

- **Titles:** Medical Writer, Scientific Writer, Regulatory Writer, Scientific Editor, Medical Editor, Publication Manager / Specialist, Medical Communications Specialist, Content Writer (Science), Grant Writer, Scientific Content Specialist.
- **Search words:** `"medical writer"`, `"scientific writer"`, `"regulatory writing"`, `"scientific editor"`, `"publications"`, `"medical communications"`, `"CSR"` (clinical study report), `"manuscript"`.
- **Where to look:** _Medical communications, HEOR & life-science consulting_ (agencies), _Pharmaceutical companies_, _Clinical research, CROs & testing labs_, academic journals and publishers, Universities and research funders (grant writers). LinkedIn is strong for agency roles; many are remote within a country.
- **Tip:** a writing test is common. Keep two or three short writing samples ready that you are allowed to share.

## Clinical research

Running clinical trials and studies with patients: coordinating visits, collecting data, monitoring sites and keeping studies compliant.

- **Titles:** Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC), Clinical Research Associate (CRA), Clinical Trial Assistant (CTA), Study Coordinator, Clinical Project Manager, Study Start-up Specialist, Clinical Data Coordinator / Manager, Research Ethics Coordinator, Regulatory Affairs Associate.
- **Search words:** `"clinical research coordinator"`, `"clinical research associate"`, `"clinical trial"`, `"GCP"`, `"study start-up"`, `"site monitoring"`, `"REB"` or `"IRB"` (research ethics board in Canada, institutional review board in the US).
- **Where to look:** _Hospitals & research institutes_ (CRC roles), _Clinical research, CROs & testing labs_ (CRA and project roles), _Pharmaceutical companies_, _Medical devices & diagnostics_. Hospital careers sites and CRO careers pages first.
- **Tip:** a Good Clinical Practice (GCP) certificate is often listed as required; free online courses exist.

## Scientific operations

Keeping labs and research organizations running: equipment, safety, inventory, quality, scheduling and project coordination.

- **Titles:** Lab Operations Manager, Research Operations Specialist, Lab Manager, Core Facility Manager / Technician, Quality Assurance / Quality Control Specialist, Biosafety Officer, Scientific Project Manager, Research Project Coordinator.
- **Search words:** `"lab operations"`, `"research operations"`, `"core facility"`, `"quality assurance" GMP` (good manufacturing practice), `"biosafety"`, `"scientific project manager"`.
- **Where to look:** _Biotech & therapeutics startups_, _Hospitals & research institutes_, _Universities & colleges_, _Clinical research, CROs & testing labs_, _Pharmaceutical companies_ (manufacturing and quality).

## Scientific software and technical support

Helping scientists use instruments, software and reagents: training customers, troubleshooting and running demos.

- **Titles:** Field Application Scientist (FAS), Application Scientist, Technical Support Scientist, Scientific Support Specialist, Product Specialist, Customer Support Scientist, Implementation Specialist, Scientific Software Trainer, Bioinformatics Support Specialist.
- **Search words:** `"field application scientist"`, `"application scientist"`, `"technical support scientist"`, `"product specialist"`, `"scientific support"`, `"implementation specialist"`.
- **Where to look:** _Scientific instruments & lab tools_, _Health tech & life-science software_, _Medical devices & diagnostics_. Mostly on employer careers pages and LinkedIn. Field roles often involve travel and a large territory.

## Solutions and sales engineering

The technical expert on a sales team: understanding a customer's problem, designing how the product solves it, giving demos and answering technical questions.

- **Titles:** Solutions Engineer, Sales Engineer, Solutions Consultant, Pre-Sales Consultant, Solutions Architect, Technical Account Manager, Scientific Solutions Consultant, Value Engineer.
- **Search words:** `"solutions engineer"`, `"sales engineer"`, `"solutions consultant"`, `"pre-sales"`, `"technical account manager"`.
- **Where to look:** _AI & large technology companies_, _Software & SaaS companies_, _Health tech & life-science software_, _Scientific instruments & lab tools_. Employer careers pages (often Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby) and LinkedIn.
- **Tip:** a domain background (science, health, finance) is a strong advantage at companies that sell into that domain.

## Customer success

Making sure customers get value after they buy: onboarding, training, renewals and acting as the customer's main contact.

- **Titles:** Customer Success Manager (CSM), Customer Success Associate, Onboarding Specialist, Implementation Manager, Account Manager, Client Services Manager, Customer Experience Specialist, Technical Account Manager.
- **Search words:** `"customer success"`, `"onboarding specialist"`, `"implementation manager"`, `"account manager"`, `"client success"`.
- **Where to look:** _Software & SaaS companies_, _AI & large technology companies_, _Health tech & life-science software_, _Banks, fintech & commerce_. LinkedIn, Indeed and employer careers pages.

## Sales (BDR, SDR and account executive)

Finding and winning new customers. Business Development Representatives (BDR) and Sales Development Representatives (SDR) are entry points: researching prospects, writing outreach and booking meetings for Account Executives (AE), who close deals.

- **Titles:** Sales Development Representative (SDR), Business Development Representative (BDR), Account Executive, Territory / Sales Representative, Inside Sales Representative, Key Account Manager, Partnerships Associate, Business Development Associate/Manager.
- **Search words:** `"sales development representative"`, `"business development representative"`, `"SDR"`, `"BDR"`, `"account executive"`, `"inside sales"`, `"territory manager"`.
- **Where to look:** _Software & SaaS companies_, _AI & large technology companies_, _Scientific instruments & lab tools_, _Medical devices & diagnostics_, _Pharmaceutical companies_ (commercial roles), _Banks, fintech & commerce_. LinkedIn and employer careers pages.
- **Tip:** postings often list on-target earnings (OTE), which combines base salary and commission. Ask how much of OTE is base pay.

## Product (AI, health and scientific products)

Deciding what a product should do and why: talking to users, setting priorities and working with engineers and designers.

- **Titles:** Product Manager, Associate Product Manager (APM), Product Owner, Technical Product Manager, Product Analyst, Product Specialist, Product Marketing Manager, Clinical Product Manager, Scientific Product Manager, AI Product Manager.
- **Search words:** `"product manager"` plus a domain (`AI`, `health`, `clinical`, `life sciences`, `scientific software`), `"associate product manager"`, `"product owner"`, `"product marketing"`.
- **Where to look:** _Health tech & life-science software_, _AI & large technology companies_, _Software & SaaS companies_, _Scientific instruments & lab tools_, _Medical devices & diagnostics_.
- **Tip:** associate and analyst roles are the usual entry point. Domain knowledge (clinical, lab, regulatory) is valued in health and scientific products.

## Strategy and operations

Making a business run better: analysis for leadership, planning, process improvement and special projects.

- **Titles:** Strategy & Operations Associate/Manager, Business Operations Analyst, Business Analyst, Chief of Staff (or Associate), Program Manager, Operations Manager, Management Consultant / Associate Consultant, Strategy Analyst, Portfolio / Project Analyst.
- **Search words:** `"strategy and operations"`, `"business operations"`, `"chief of staff"`, `"business analyst"`, `"program manager"`, `"associate consultant"`.
- **Where to look:** _Consulting & market research_, _Medical communications, HEOR & life-science consulting_, _Banks, fintech & commerce_, _Software & SaaS companies_, _Pharmaceutical companies_ (commercial and business-planning teams).

## Data and analytics

Turning data into answers: cleaning and analyzing data, building dashboards and reports, and sometimes building models.

- **Titles:** Data Analyst, Business Intelligence (BI) Analyst, Research Analyst, Health Data Analyst, Epidemiologist, Biostatistician, Real-World Evidence (RWE) Analyst, HEOR Analyst, Data Scientist, Bioinformatician, Analytics Consultant.
- **Search words:** `"data analyst"`, `"research analyst"`, `"health data"`, `"real-world evidence"`, `"HEOR"` (health economics and outcomes research), `"biostatistician"`, `"epidemiologist"`, `"data scientist"`, plus tools you know (`SQL`, `R`, `Python`, `Tableau`, `Power BI`).
- **Where to look:** _Hospitals & research institutes_, _Government & public agencies_, _Clinical research, CROs & testing labs_, _Medical communications, HEOR & life-science consulting_, _Banks, fintech & commerce_, _Software & SaaS companies_, _Consulting & market research_.

## Academic and postdoctoral

Research and teaching jobs at universities and research institutes, from training positions to faculty.

- **Titles:** Postdoctoral Fellow / Researcher, Research Associate (academic), Research Fellow, Lecturer, Sessional / Contract Instructor, Teaching Stream Faculty, Assistant Professor, Research Scientist (academic), Program Coordinator (academic).
- **Search words:** `"postdoctoral"`, `"postdoc"`, `"research fellow"`, `"assistant professor"` plus your field, `"lecturer"`, `"sessional instructor"`, `"teaching stream"`.
- **Where to look:** University faculty and postdoctoral job pages (often separate from staff jobs), _Hospitals & research institutes_, University Affairs (Canada), HigherEdJobs (US), Nature Careers, Science Careers, Times Higher Education Jobs, jobs.ac.uk, and society job boards in your field. Many postdoctoral positions are only advertised on lab websites, mailing lists or by word of mouth, so contacting principal investigators directly is common.
- **Tip:** academic positions are often fixed-term. Read the contract length, funding source and renewal terms in the posting.

## Building your search

1. Pick one to three lanes.
2. From each lane, pick three to five titles that match your experience level.
3. Make one saved search per title on one or two boards, plus a weekly check of your top employers' careers pages.
4. When a job looks right, open the employer's own posting, check the requisition number, and ask your assistant to add it to your job list.
